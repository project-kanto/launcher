# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Kanto players installing or updating the legacy mobile client. Many are unfamiliar with IPA/APK patching, Apple signing, USB trust prompts, or 32-bit Android compatibility. They may arrive from Discord already frustrated by manual setup and need the launcher to make the next action obvious.

## Product Purpose

Kanto Launcher verifies the supported original game, applies Kanto's published update locally, and guides installation on Android or a USB-connected iPhone. Success means a player reaches the game without needing command-line knowledge or repeated Discord support. The launcher also surfaces Kanto server and game-update status when players return.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, playful, competent. The voice is friendly and direct without sounding childish, corporate, or overly technical. Kanto's warm field-guide identity should be recognisable, while the interface itself behaves like a trusted native utility.

## Anti-references

- PowerPoint-like stacks of large cards and explanatory copy.
- Generic AI-generated SaaS layouts and oversized slogans.
- Glass and gradients applied to every surface without hierarchy.
- Dense gamer launchers full of promotions or ornamental telemetry.
- Wizard screens that hide current status or make users remember earlier steps.

## Design Principles

- Make the launcher feel alive: Home carries news, server status and community links; Library owns installation and updates.
- Use a soft atmospheric shell with restrained translucent depth, keeping the strongest contrast on useful actions.
- Keep the journey visible: players should always know what is ready, what is happening, and what they need to do next.
- Preserve confidence: verify before modifying, explain failures plainly, and keep safe recovery actions beside the problem.
- Give Kanto its own launcher identity instead of reproducing the website layout.
- Keep support friction low: progressive disclosure for Apple credentials, verification, device limits, and compatibility guidance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, provide complete keyboard focus and labels, pair status colours with text or icons, respect reduced motion, and keep controls comfortably tappable. The supported desktop window must not require page scrolling; compact layouts may use bounded internal overflow only as a recovery path.
